Spreadsheet workarounds

When the system can’t do something, a spreadsheet fills the gap. Over time those sheets multiply, get copied and emailed around, and quietly become part of how the business runs.

What it costs you

  • No one is sure which version is current
  • Formula errors and typos go unnoticed
  • Reporting means stitching files together by hand

Duplicated records

The same customer, supplier or product is stored in several places, each updated by different people at different times. Eventually the copies disagree and nobody knows which to trust.

What it costs you

  • Invoices and deliveries go to out-of-date details
  • Time is lost checking and reconciling records
  • Reports don’t match across departments

Manual exports

Because the old system can’t share data automatically, someone has to export it, reformat it and load it somewhere else. It’s routine, easy to get wrong and depends on one person remembering to do it.

What it costs you

  • Skilled people spend hours on copy-and-paste work
  • Data is out of date the moment it’s exported
  • One missed step breaks the reports downstream

Disconnected tools

Newer tools have been added around the old system, but none of them share information. People become the integration — copying details from one screen to another and chasing updates by email.

What it costs you

  • No single, reliable view of customers or orders
  • Handoffs between teams are slow and error-prone
  • Subscriptions overlap while gaps remain
